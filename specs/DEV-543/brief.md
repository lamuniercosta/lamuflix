## Scoring and reasons

1. Load DEV-544 library features once per request and derive seeds and candidates from that load. Candidates are unwatched and not disliked within the existing eligibility boundaries. A watchlisted movie can be both a candidate and a positive seed. Map both sides of DEV-518 similarity consistently, including full cast, keywords and collection.
2. Compare each candidate with each positive seed except itself. The default positive weights are liked **1.0**, watched without review **0.6**, and watchlist **0.4**. Use the existing decay calculation and age semantics through the shared helper for seeds with playback. Keep the weights, rank multipliers, disliked weight and decay floor in recommendation options.
3. The positive term sums the top three products of DEV-518 similarity and effective seed weight, with default rank multipliers **1**, **0.5**, and **0.25**. Sum the available products if there are fewer than three. Subtract the highest similarity to a disliked seed multiplied by the configured disliked weight, default **1.0**. A library without disliked seeds contributes zero penalty.
4. Qualify on at least one positive pair with raw similarity at or above the shared bar or a shared strong reason, before weighting, recency, aggregation and the disliked penalty. Rank qualifying candidates by the signed aggregate, including weak pairs in the top three when they rank there. Select the card seed from all strong positive pairs, even if the best strong pair falls outside the three contributions used in the score. A page may contain fewer than the requested count.
5. Keep the selected seed's movie ID, title and signal kind with its DEV-518 evidence for reason construction. The endpoint sends the seed line first in the existing `reasons` list, followed by the DEV-518 evidence for that same pair. Keep the templates together in the reason builder and mirror this table in the docs. OpenAPI and generated client types keep the existing shape.

| Signal | Seed reason |
| --- | --- |
| Liked | Because you liked {title} |
| Watched without review | Because you watched {title} |
| Watchlist | Because {title} is on your watchlist |
