## Decision 1 — IMDb eligibility

- **Decision**: Reject when **any known** IMDb value is below its inclusive floor (rating < 6.5 or votes < 1,000), even when the counterpart is unknown or absent. A known floor failure with unknown votes is complete known-below-floor evidence and is stored for 30 days. Evaluate known floor failures and confirmed absence before the unresolved/no-row rule. A valid rating at or above 6.5 with unknown votes is unresolved for the vote floor and defers. Confirmed absent IMDb rating/ID (explicit provider-confirmed absence per recon null / “Movie not found!” mapping, lines 5–8) preserves baseline eligibility. Transient failure, deferred lookup, and malformed/transient values are unresolved — not confirmed absence — with no durable negative; budget continues; original DEV-561 position restored on resolution. Exact Patron: "Unresolved, transient-failure, deferred, and unknown-votes evidence gets no durable entry and is retried later under Q1." Durable expiry: 30 days complete (including known floor failures), 7 days confirmed absence, none for unresolved.
- **Rationale**: Wanted 1 and 4; recon null-rating / not-found mapping (`OmdbMetadataProvider.Mapping.cs` / unit test named at recon lines 5–8); Patron Q1.
- **Alternatives considered**: Treat missing votes as floor failure; durable-negative cache on transient failure; require both values known before rejecting — rejected by Patron Q1 / analyze repair precedence.

## Decision 2 — Visible-row scan, collection, and budget

- **Decision**: Walk DEV-561 order **serially** until 20 **admitted** collection representatives or candidates exhausted; retain candidates beyond any former top-20 cut-off for backfill; first admitted title represents its collection; skip known duplicates before lookup; use fresh cached membership until expiry unless fresher local membership is known; recheck membership before admission when collection becomes known mid-resolution; live IMDb resolution only for open-slot candidates; **per-load ceiling fixed at load start**; per-load live OMDb attempts and shared allowance both enforced at the **individual outbound-send boundary** (including retry with one slot remaining); each attempt reserves for its current UTC date; midnight does not replenish the load ceiling; send-boundary denial defers without outbound send and without failing the load; coalesced waiters send/charge nothing; rejections do not replenish; after exhaustion or denial use cache/confirmed absence only (no fresh resolution); cache hits and known-duplicate skips make zero outbound calls to **both** providers; TMDb `external_ids` only when an OMDb send could follow, counted even when failed/empty, no extra budget/TTL; shared atomic reservation stays independent of the load cap; shorter row allowed.
- **Rationale**: Wanted 1–4; recon ranking/cap at `OutsideLibraryRanking.cs` and DEV-561 spec (recon lines 15–18); Patron Q2; Phase 2 F1/F2/F5/F9/F10.
- **Alternatives considered**: Cutting the candidate pool at 20 before backfill; per-request or per-feature OMDb pools; replenishing per-load attempts after rejection; speculative parallel lookups; charging coalesced waiters; extra TMDb TTL/budget — rejected by Patron Q2 / Phase 2.

## Decision 4 — Rollout order

- **Decision**: Environments with existing OMDb use: candidate-evidence migration → provision and read back positive allowance (≤ provider plan from shared use) → deploy revised guard. Environments without OMDb use may migrate and remain without a usable live allowance until configured. Prove enrichment through guarded adapter and two consumers on one pool with failures charged. No code bypass for invalid configuration. No default/fallback/bypass allowance.
- **Rationale**: Wanted 2; Q2–Q3 / Q3–Q4 update; Patron Q4.
- **Alternatives considered**: Silent default allowance (including keeping 1000); enable revised guard before provision — rejected by Patron Q4 / Q3–Q4 update.
