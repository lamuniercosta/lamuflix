## Domain vocabulary *(mandatory — spec-local clarification of existing ticket/brief meanings only; no CONTEXT.md expansion)*

**IMDb quality floor**
Inclusive minimums on known IMDb evidence: rating 6.5 and vote count 1,000. **Any known** value below either floor rejects the candidate, even when the other value is unknown or absent.
_Avoid_: treating missing evidence as a floor failure; treating unresolved/deferred lookups as rejections; requiring both values to be known before a known failure can reject

**Confirmed absence**
Explicit provider-confirmed missing IMDb rating or ID (recon: null mapping / “Movie not found!” distinction, lines 5–8) that preserves baseline eligibility and may be stored with a 7-day freshness window.
_Avoid_: writing confirmed absence for malformed or transient failures; treating absence as a floor rejection

**Unresolved evidence**
Transient lookup failure, deferred lookup, malformed/transient provider values, or valid at/above-floor rating with unknown votes — not a rejection, no durable negative entry, candidate may be deferred while budget continues.
_Avoid_: durable negative cache for unresolved outcomes; permanently dropping the candidate’s DEV-561 rank position; conflating unresolved with confirmed absence

**Collection representative**
The first admitted eligible title for a known positive TMDb collection ID in DEV-561 order; later known members of that collection are skipped without lookup; membership rechecked before admission if collection becomes known during evidence resolution.
_Avoid_: inventing a collection for titles that have none; looking up known duplicates after a representative is admitted; cutting the candidate pool at 20 before backfill

**Shared OMDb daily allowance**
One application-wide finite daily request budget shared by every OMDb consumer, request, and user, reserved atomically by UTC calendar date before each outbound attempt via the landed gate/store; independent of the per-load live-attempt cap.
_Avoid_: per-feature, per-user, or per-request pools; charging cache hits or coalesced waiters; enabling the revised guard without a provisioned positive allowance in environments that already use OMDb; inventing a second quota table

Consumed vocabulary (already in use; not redefined): **Not in your library yet**, discovery seed, discovery card, DEV-561 ranking (liked 2 / watched 1, score desc, TMDb ID asc, cap 20).

**Why this priority**: Wanted 1 and 4 — the primary quality filter on the existing row.

**Independent Test**: Unit/property tests cover exact 6.5 and 1,000 boundaries and immediately below; mixed-evidence cases (known failing rating with unknown votes; known failing votes with unknown rating); numeric parsing fixtures; confirmed absence vs malformed/transient vs unresolved; deferred candidates restore original sort position when evidence resolves; no durable negative for unresolved outcomes. Early floor checkpoints remain fixture-testable while production live wiring waits on the guard and cache.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a candidate with known IMDb rating 6.5 and known votes 1,000, **When** eligibility is evaluated, **Then** it passes both floors.
2. **Given** a candidate with any known rating below 6.5 or any known votes below 1,000 (including when the counterpart is unknown or absent), **When** eligibility is evaluated, **Then** it is rejected and does not appear in the visible row.
3. **Given** a valid rating at or above 6.5 with unknown votes, **When** eligibility is evaluated, **Then** the candidate is unresolved for the vote floor and deferred (not rejected).
4. **Given** confirmed absent IMDb rating or ID and no failing known value, **When** eligibility is evaluated, **Then** baseline DEV-561 eligibility is preserved.
5. **Given** unresolved evidence (transient failure, deferred lookup, malformed/transient values, or valid rating with unknown votes), **When** the load proceeds, **Then** the candidate is not rejected, no durable negative entry is written, lower-ranked candidates may fill open slots within budget, and the deferred candidate returns to its original sort position when evidence later resolves.

### Edge Cases

- Rating exactly 6.5 or votes exactly 1,000 → pass.
- Rating or votes immediately below the floor → reject.
- Any known failing value with unknown/absent counterpart → reject (decisive known failure).
- Valid rating at/above 6.5 with unknown votes → unresolved (defer); not a rejection.
- Transient OMDb/TMDb failure or malformed provider value → unresolved; no durable negative; not confirmed absence.
- Confirmed “not found” / absent IMDb ID or rating → eligible unless a known value fails; 7-day freshness.
- Known floor failure stored as complete evidence → 30-day freshness; still rejected while fresh.
- Expired evidence → treated as missing for live/cache purposes and may be refreshed within budget.
- Quota exhausted or denied mid-load after some rejections → rejections do not replenish the fixed per-load ceiling; send-boundary denial defers without outbound send and without failing the load; further backfill uses cache/confirmed absence only; a lower cached survivor may still admit.
- UTC calendar-date rollover → each attempt reserves for its current UTC date from injectable time seam; crossing midnight does not replenish the current load's ceiling or restart a cache-only load; no background job required.
- Concurrent identical misses → one in-process leader on a shared Infrastructure instance charges and completes the full miss chain; waiters reuse the outcome with zero send/charge and never promote; leader failure/cancellation defers waiters and permits later-load retry after cleanup; waiter cancellation is independent; cross-process quota still uses atomic reservation independent of the load cap.
- Malformed IMDb request ID before send → defer with zero OMDb sends and no usage increment; malformed ID echoed by OMDb after a send remains charged as unresolved.
- TMDb `external_ids` with no following OMDb room, prior denial, or no remaining per-load TMDb ceiling room → no call; failed/empty external-ID outcomes still count against the per-load TMDb ceiling.
- Titles without collection → never blocked by collection dedupe.
- Fresh cached collection membership → used until expiry unless fresher local membership is already known; no remote refresh solely to check a fresh cache hit.
- Collection discovered mid-resolution → recheck before admission.
- Candidate beyond former top-20 cut-off → retained for backfill into open admitted slots.
- Discovery loading/empty/error → section hidden; library grid usable.
- Cached/confirmed-absent fill with live OMDb disabled → display prerequisites still support visible survivors.
- Dead code orphaned solely by this change → deleted in the same PR before gate runs.
