## Q3 — Delivery, failure, and cancellation

**Keel question:** [from Keel] DEV-330 Q2 appended and read back. Do you approve that AC1 means one broadcast invocation per transition rather than guaranteed client receipt, and the failure/cancellation policy? **Patron answer (verbatim):**
[from Patron] Q3 ruling — Approved (design ruling, not blocked): AC1 means one awaited post-commit broadcast invocation per committed status transition with ephemeral delivery, no durable notification outbox or replay, logged-and-swallowed send failures so a committed save never fails or retries enrichment, and post-commit invocation that does not skip the broadcast solely because the caller request token canceled after commit.
- ADR-0022 (docs/adr/0022-signalr-enrichment-status-push.md:15) explicitly fixes single-instance in-memory SignalR push with no backplane and specifies that on reconnect the client refetches pending movies rather than replaying missed events.
- Ticket DEV-330 (note DEV-330:27-30) fixes its design by ADR-0022 and requires one EnrichmentStatusChanged per committed change and no message on failed save; isolating post-commit broadcast exceptions prevents corrupting already-committed database state or triggering duplicate enrichment work in ProcessEnrichmentCommandHandler (ProcessEnrichmentCommandHandler.cs:89-105, 151-155).
- Cancellation before db.SaveChangesAsync/transaction commit throws before persistence and emits nothing (satisfying AC2), whereas once the commit succeeds the status transition is durable and the post-commit broadcast attempt must run (e.g. with CancellationToken.None) rather than silently dropping the notification due to post-commit caller token cancellation.

**Implications:** No delivery guarantee across process crashes or disconnected clients. One awaited attempt follows a successful commit; send exceptions are logged and isolated from persistence outcome. This is Patron interpretation of the accepted ADR, not a scope reduction. SignalR client reconnection remains DEV-422.
