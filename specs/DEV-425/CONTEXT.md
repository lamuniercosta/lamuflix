# Director filtering context

Vocabulary for choosing movies by a credited director.

## Language

**Director**: A person credited with directing a movie. A director identity is distinct from an actor identity, even when their names match.
_Avoid_: Cast member, actor.

**Director facet**: The list of directors credited on at least one library movie, with the number of library movies credited to each director.
_Avoid_: People facet, cast list.

**Director filter**: A choice of one director that limits the movie list to movies credited to that director.
_Avoid_: Director multi-select, cast filter.
