## Settled decisions

- **Opening defaults.** Group the initial ranked response on the client. Define the maximum of four liked/watched seed rows and six cards per themed row together in one file. Use “Close to your watchlist” for watchlist seeds. Build “Next in a series you started” and hide it when empty. Basis: `ticket-text.txt:28-30,52,54,79-83`.
- **Q1 — series signal.** Under the proposed amendment, `StartedCollectionId` is an optional nullable `int?` on the shared `RecommendedMovieDto`. Populate it on For You only for a candidate in a collection with at least one watched movie in the library. Its identity lets the client pick at most one candidate per collection from the ranked response. Basis: `ticket-text.txt:19,28,54,74,83,88` and `recon-DEV-538:51-53`.
- **Q2 — seed presence.** Under the same amendment, the existing For You page envelope becomes `{ items, hasMore, hasSeeds }`. `hasSeeds` is required and true if at least one eligible liked, watched, or watchlist seed exists before candidate selection, strong-match filtering, caps, paging, or swap exclusions. Supply false otherwise. Required on every successful For You response. Basis: `ticket-text.txt:36-39,49,54` and `recon-DEV-538:51,54`.
- **Q3 — assignment and order.** Use the DEV-543 ranked, strong-match set. Deduplicate by movie ID and render the series row first. For each non-null started collection ID, select the earliest known release year, then use API rank to break ties. Unknown years follow known years. Sort the series row by the same comparator. Route candidates not selected for the series row by their single best seed. Group liked and watched candidates by seed movie ID and reported signal, then label them “Because you liked {title}” or “Because you watched {title}”. Choose up to four groups with at least two candidates, by descending candidate count and then first API appearance. Place the watchlist row after the seed rows and More for you last. Preserve API order within seed and watchlist rows and in More for you. Fold singleton themed rows directly into More for you, including a singleton series row. Also send unselected seed groups and cards above the six-card themed-row cap to More for you. More for you has no themed-row cap and can contain a single card. Basis: `ticket-text.txt:25-35,79-83`.
- **Q4 — paging, Swap, and empty responses.** Compute themed rows and the initial More for you bucket from one initial GET. Keep themed rows fixed across More for you actions. Apply established DEV-517 request semantics to Load more and Swap, excluding and deduplicating against movie IDs across all rendered rows. An empty follow-up preserves the rendered cards. Rule 6 applies only after a successful initial GET with zero items. Basis: `ticket-text.txt:31-39,48-49,54` and `recon-DEV-538:23,45`.
- **Q5 — reachable controls.** If More for you has zero cards and `hasMore` is true, render that labelled region with its `h2` and Load more. Mount its carousel once it has a card. Offer Swap when the existing DEV-517 prerequisites hold and omit More for you when it has neither cards nor an available control. Basis: `ticket-text.txt:33,35,40,44,48,50,54`.
- **Q6 — empty-state copy.** Case 6a (`hasSeeds === false`): “No recommendations yet. Mark movies as watched or liked to get some.” Case 6b (`hasSeeds === true`): “No recommendations closely match the movies you have liked, watched, or added to your watchlist yet.” Both use “Browse the library” linking to the existing library destination. Basis: `ticket-text.txt:20,36-39,49`.
- **Q7 — delivery sequence.** Confirm the landed prerequisite contracts, then implement and verify the backend response, regenerate the web contract, build the grouped UI, and collect gate and acceptance evidence. Preserve Related JSON by setting `StartedCollectionId` only in the For You projection and using `JsonIgnoreCondition.WhenWritingNull` on the shared DTO. The wire field may be omitted when null and the generated TypeScript is expected to expose an optional nullable number. Basis: `ticket-text.txt:25-54,63-75,87-89` and `recon-DEV-538:21-34,51-54`.
- **Q8 — review terms.** Patron ratified the owner-conditional scope, evidence bar, two review rounds, and at most two fix commits per round. The consolidated checkbox gates Gate 1 and the spec brief proceeds. Basis: `task-pipeline:18-29` and Q1–Q7.
