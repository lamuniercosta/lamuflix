# ADR-0002. Explicit handlers and decorators instead of MediatR

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-24
- Ticket: DEV-292 (parent DEV-281)

## Context

Every use case needs a handler and a place for cross-cutting behaviour. MediatR, pipeline behaviours, reflection scanning, and source-generated buses were the dispatch mechanisms rejected for that role.

## Decision

Use explicit handlers and hand-wired decorators. Do not use MediatR.

Every use case is an `ICommandHandler<TCommand, TResult>` or `IQueryHandler<TQuery, TResult>` in its feature folder. Commands and queries are `sealed record`s. Handlers are `sealed`. Cross-cutting behaviour is registered explicitly through `services.AddHandler<THandler, TCommand, TResult>()`. Decorator order, outer to inner, is Tracing, then Logging, then Validation, then the handler. No other mechanism may dispatch handlers.

Explicit registration makes the call chain readable in one file, keeps decorators unit-testable without a framework, and matches the original reference implementation.

## Consequences

- New use cases are sealed commands or queries with sealed handlers registered via `AddHandler`.
- MediatR, pipeline behaviours, reflection scanning, and source-generated buses stay out of handler dispatch.
